# Loopsmith privacy policy

Loopsmith does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.

- **What is stored:** the loops you create (start, end, how many times they play, whether they are
  switched on) for each video or track, and your preferences. They are kept in your browser's local
  extension storage, on your device only.
- **What is sent:** nothing. Loopsmith makes no network requests of its own and contains no
  analytics, tracking or advertising.
- **Website access:** Loopsmith runs on a fixed list of media sites (YouTube, YouTube Music, Spotify,
  SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Vimeo, Twitch, Bilibili, Dailymotion) and on other sites only after you turn
  it on for them. There it reads the state of the media player (position, duration, which item is
  loaded) to repeat sections; it does not read or keep any other page content.
- **Diagnostics:** the popup's *Copy diagnostics* button shows a short report (version, browser,
  site, and whether a player was found) and copies it to your clipboard when you click it. It is
  never sent by Loopsmith; you decide whether to paste it into a bug report.
- **Removing your data:** uninstalling Loopsmith deletes everything it stored.

Questions: https://github.com/noahbds/loopsmith/issues
